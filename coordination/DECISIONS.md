Exact neglect thresholds, warnings, caps, offline-time behavior, and whether inactive animals can lose friendship remain **unresolved design questions**, not authorized engine behavior. | 2026-11-21 (sanctuary, hospital, active-play neglect) | **Owner decision:** all healthy owned animals share a central pen; sick animals move to a hospital bay and return when recovered. Pen dirtiness develops and creates cleaning chores. Animal frustration/discomfort provides progressive behavioral warnings before any friendship decrease. **Inactivity/offline time must never advance neglect**; only active gameplay may count toward neglect. Dirtiness alone is not neglect and must not automatically lower friendship. Any owned animal, including inactive companions, can eventually lose friendship from sustained unaddressed sick/dirty needs **during active gameplay**, after warnings. Exact active-play definition, thresholds, disease triggers, decay amounts, and hospital/cleaning mechanics require design review before implementation. Existing no-death/no-removal rule remains. |
| 2026-11-21 (neglect escalation by informed choice) | **Owner decision:** neglect/friendship loss is triggered by **informed, repeated choices**, not mere time, ordinary adventures, unseen mess, or absence. If they then leave that need unresolved and proceed to another musical adventure, a small friendship deduction can occur. On subsequent visits, if the same need is visibly presented and the learner again chooses to leave it unresolved for another adventure, the deduction increases progressively, with increasingly expressive animal frustration. Providing appropriate care stops escalation and resets that neglect sequence; friendship gains still come only from musical adventures with the active companion. No penalties without prior awareness or during idle/offline time. Exact deduction schedule, maximum, issue attribution across animals, and warning presentation remain design/test proposals. |
