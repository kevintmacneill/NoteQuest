NOTEQUEST — MOCK PET CARE INTERACTION

Care for your companion

Bathing the animal and cleaning its enclosure are separate tasks. This demonstration has only sample in-memory state; no real game saves or coins are touched.

The actual NoteQuest app must call verified `NQ.pets`, `NQ.pens`, `NQ.shop`, and `NQ.companions` methods. This demo cannot be used as evidence that those APIs work or that any pet is saved.
