## 4. Two ways to fit the series together (Kevin hasn't chosen)

### Option A: one concept spine with age tracks
The **worlds follow one shared concept order** (the spine = Alfred's leveling above). Each learner has a **track** that changes **pacing, presentation and amount of review**, never the mastery bar (Kevin's 2026-11-27 universal-metrics rule):
- **Prep track** (young children, e.g. Zoe): about one Prep book per world half, with more stops and more review.
- **Basic track** (about 7–9, e.g. Micaela after Prep A).
- **Complete track** (older beginners): the same concepts, fewer review items, faster.
- **Adult track** (Kevin, Erika): the Adult All-in-One's order and adult presentation.

A possible world map, following Alfred's own leveling (two Prep books ≈ one Basic level):

| World | Spine stage | Prep track | Basic track | Alfred skill level |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Melody Harbor | first half of 1A | Prep A (decided) | 1A, part 1 | Early Elementary |
| 2 | rest of 1A | Prep B | 1A, part 2 | Early Elementary |
| 3 | 1B | Prep C + D | 1B | Elementary |
| 4 | 2 | Prep E + F | 2 | Late Elementary |
| 5 | 3 | (switch to Basic 3, per Alfred's arrow) | 3 | Early Intermediate |
| 6 | 4 | (none) | 4 | Intermediate |
| 7 | 5 | (none) | 5 | Intermediate |
| 8 | 6 | (none) | 6 | Late Intermediate |
| 9 Grand Music City | beyond Alfred Level 6 | (none) | (none) | RCM yardstick (Level 7 and up) |

Pros: one curriculum to build and test; every learner of any age shares worlds, which suits a family sharing a tablet. Cons: the track logic (pace and review per track) is extra engine work; worlds 1–2 are short on the Basic track.

### Option B: worlds = Basic Piano Library levels
Worlds map straight onto Basic 1A, 1B, 2…6 (worlds 1–7), with worlds 8–9 beyond Alfred. Prep, Complete and Adult are used only as cross-checks.

Pros: simplest; matches one book series exactly. Cons: **it conflicts with the decided "Harbor = Prep A"** (Harbor would become all of 1A, intervals, rests and staccato included); young children get no slower Prep pacing; adult ordering is ignored.

**Claude's lean (for Kevin to accept or reject):** Option A, because Kevin's "same mastery bar, different presentation" decision already describes it, and Harbor = Prep A already fits as World 1.
