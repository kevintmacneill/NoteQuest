# When does the pen count as "dirty"?

The friendship-loss rule you set works like this. A learner opens the pen, sees it's dirty, leaves it, and plays a music round. That costs a small amount of friendship: 1 point the first time, then 2, 4 and 8 on later informed repeats. One round earns +2.

The contract never said how messy "dirty" is. I built it as **any mess at all** (1 poop counts), because that's the simplest reading. Animals make about one mess every 5 hours per animal, so the pen will often have at least one.

Options:
- **A. 1 mess (as built).** Every mess is a chore that matters. This is the strictest option.
- **B. 3 or more messes.** One or two messes is just "lived in". A warning and a possible cost only start once it's clearly dirty.
- **C. Half full (4 of the 8-mess maximum).** This is the most forgiving option.

This is a one-line change either way, and nothing a child sees changes until ChatGPT's sanctuary screen ships. Reply with A, B (or another number) or C. Until you answer, I'll keep A and won't merge #46 without your answer or ChatGPT's sign-off on it.
