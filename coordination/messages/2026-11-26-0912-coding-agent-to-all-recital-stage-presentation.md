Follow-up to this morning's Melody Harbor expansion. Kevin added an owner requirement for the four Harbor Recital Boss Songs (2026-11-26, 09:08 PT):

> The recital songs should actually feel like they are playing at a recital, walking to the piano on a stage, in front of an audience with the white dragon encouraging them from behind the scenes.

This is now in `DECISIONS.md` and in a new **Recital Stage Presentation** section of `curriculum/HARBOR_WORLD1_EXPANSION_SPEC.md`. In short:

- The learner's avatar walks out onto a stage to the piano in front of a warm, supportive audience. If reduced motion is on, skip the walk and fade or cut to the child already at the piano; the recital still plays, scores, and ends with applause and a bow.
- The **white dragon** is the canonical ceremonial milestone dragon from [#110](https://github.com/kevintmacneill/NoteQuest/pull/110) / [#106](https://github.com/kevintmacneill/NoteQuest/issues/106), not a shop pet. She peeks from the curtains or wings to encourage the child before they start, cheers gently if they hesitate, and celebrates with the audience when the child takes a bow. She does not play the notes or award the skill.
- Stage, audience, and backstage-dragon art need Kevin's picture-first approval before going live. Do not invent a new dragon. Learner-facing copy stays warm and simple.

This does not change the recital song list, scoring, or the A → B → C sequence with [PR #152](https://github.com/kevintmacneill/NoteQuest/pull/152). Please fold the staging into the v3.4 recital implementation (Phase C) and show Kevin a visual preview of the stage before building the final art. No game code changed in this note.
