# One sanctuary question for you: should pets get sick or the pen get messy while nobody is playing?

ChatGPT and I settled everything else in the companion/sanctuary plan (v0.5). This is the one point that changes something you already decided (D2), so it's yours.

**Today (D2, already in PR #6):** pets' needs drop in real time. After about 2 days of very low care a pet gets sick, and messes pile up while the tablet is off.

**Under the new rules,** being away never costs friendship either way. The question is only what the learner **finds** when they come back after a few days:

- **A. Active play only** *(ChatGPT and I both recommend)*. Needs, mess and sickness move only while the game is open and being played (stopping after 5 minutes with no taps). Come back after a week and the sanctuary is exactly as you left it. Fits your "don't punish not playing", since finding a pet in the hospital feels like a punishment to a 5- or 7-year-old even if no friendship is lost. Pets would get sick after a stretch of *play time* without care instead of 2 days; we'd propose the number before building.
- **B. Keep real time** (as D2 works now). More like a real pet, and simpler, but the girls can come back to a sick pet and a messy pen.
